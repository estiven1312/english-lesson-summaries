# Sources and Reading Notes

This register records what was read and where the grammar comes from. Links open the original local PDFs in Obsidian when the PDFs and notes are inside the same vault. Page numbers are **PDF page numbers**, not screenshot crop numbers.

The five screenshot PDFs were read visually from rendered pages. The tall pages were inspected in overlapping sections. Text extraction was used for the 22-page idioms PDF. Embedded lesson audio was not played.

The standalone August PNG in the workspace was outside the requested PDF set and was not included.

## Reading conventions

- **Source example** or **source wording** identifies wording directly visible in a PDF.
- **New example**, **original**, or **study extension** identifies added teaching material.
- Explanations paraphrase the lessons and clarify grammar; these notes are not full transcriptions.
- Secondary grammar connections are labelled where a lesson illustrates them without teaching them as its main target.
- Missing text is flagged, not silently filled in.
