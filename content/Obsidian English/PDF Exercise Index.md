## What comes from the PDFs?

- Sections marked **PDF** reproduce or lightly format visible tasks.
- Sections marked **adapted** change the task format or provide explicitly added prompts.
- Sections marked **added** are new practice based on the PDFs' language.
- Source locations appear at the top of each note.
- Answers are suggested solutions, not an official publisher answer key. Multiple valid answers are explained where needed.
- Static PDFs do not provide playable audio. Listening tasks use visible text where possible; inferred matches are labelled.
- The cut-off bottom sections of two PDFs have not been invented.
