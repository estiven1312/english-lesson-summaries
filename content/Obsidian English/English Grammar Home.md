# English Grammar Home

Start here. These notes connect the grammar in your five screenshot PDFs and selected idioms from the sixth PDF.

## How to use this in Obsidian

Open the **content** folder as a vault, then open this note. Alternatively, copy **Obsidian English** and **PDFs** together into an existing vault, keeping their folder structure so the source links work. No plugins are required.

The notes explain grammar in simple English. **Source examples** are short quotations from a PDF; **new examples** and **study extensions** were written for these notes. These are study notes, not full transcriptions. See [[Sources and Reading Notes]] for exact files, page references, corrections, and missing content.
