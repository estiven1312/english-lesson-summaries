## E. Choose the form or meaning - added practice

1. “Can all those suggestions really be correct?” probably means:
   A. Every suggestion must be correct.
   B. Some suggestions are probably incorrect.
   C. Please count the suggestions.
2. Why ______ they assume the AI catches everything?
   A. do B. does C. are
3. Do most managers really ______ that claim?
   A. believes B. believing C. believe
4. Who ______ time to audit every output?
   A. does have B. has C. do has
5. “Isn't it kind of reckless?” most likely expresses:
   A. agreement that the action is safe B. concern about the risk C. a request for a schedule

## Answers

### A - possible answer

They worry that people accept AI output and claims too readily, without critically checking results, understanding limitations, or reviewing the generated code.

### B

1. **I mean, why do they assume the AI catches everything?**
2. **Do most engineering managers really believe that stuff?**

The introductory *I mean* is optional when identifying the question itself.

### C - text-based suggested matches

**1-B, 2-D, 3-C, 4-A.**

- 1-B criticizes adopting a tool without understanding its limits.
- 2-D emphasizes how hard it is to keep up with rapidly changing tools.
- 3-C questions why foundational knowledge is not taught.
- 4-A suggests evaluating tools more carefully.

These are contextual inferences, not audio-verified answers.

### D - original sample response

I agree with “Why don't teams slow down and evaluate?” A small trial can help a team find limitations before depending on a tool. For example, we could compare its suggestions with our own tests before changing the whole workflow.

Other reasoned opinions are valid; agreement is not required.

### E

1. **B** - the question expresses skepticism.
2. **A** - plural *they* takes *do*.
3. **C** - use the base verb after *do*.
4. **B** - *who* is the subject, so a neutral subject question needs no added *do*.
5. **B** - the speaker considers it risky.
