# 06 IELTS Expressions Exercises

Source: [[Sources and Reading Notes#S6 - Useful idioms for IELTS|S6]], pp. 5-6 and selected expressions on pp. 7-20. [[PDFs/IELTS/Common Expressions/IELTS common idioms.pdf|Open PDF]]

Return to [[PDF Exercise Index]]. All answers are at the end of this note. Suggested solutions were prepared for these notes; they are not an official publisher answer key.

The PDF contains idiom explanations and question-and-answer examples, rather than a dedicated exercise worksheet. **Section A uses its actual speaking questions; sections B-D are added exercises based on its expressions.** Sample answers are new.

## A. Speaking questions from the PDF - pp. 5-6

Answer in two or three sentences. Suggested expressions are added prompts; use them only where they fit naturally.

1. How well do you get on with your friends? **(make my blood boil / see eye to eye)**
2. Can you tell us something about your hometown? **(have a whale of a time)**
3. Tell us about one habit of yours that everyone should have. **(on the dot)**
4. Why did you take up this particular subject? **(find my feet)**
5. How would you describe your parents? **(set in their ways)**
6. What was the reason behind having such good academic scores? **(burn the midnight oil)**
7. How many colleges/universities did you apply to? **(down in the dumps)**
8. What did you do on your last birthday? **(over the moon)**

## B. Choose the meaning - added exercise

1. **Have a whale of a time** means:
   A. travel by sea B. enjoy yourself very much C. work late
2. **Find your feet** means:
   A. become comfortable in a new situation B. buy shoes C. lose confidence
3. **Cost an arm and a leg** means:
   A. be very cheap B. be dangerous C. be very expensive
4. **Once in a blue moon** means:
   A. very often B. very rarely C. exactly on time
5. **Pull your weight** means:
   A. do your fair share B. lift something heavy C. leave early
6. **Take it with a grain of salt** means:
   A. accept it completely B. add seasoning C. treat it skeptically
7. **Under the weather** means:
   A. outside B. unwell C. excited
8. **A piece of cake** describes something:
   A. very easy B. very expensive C. very confusing

## D. Choose the grammatical form - added exercise

1. She finally found ______ feet. A) his B) her C) their
2. That advertisement ______ my eye yesterday. A) caught B) catch C) catching

## Answers

### A - original model answers

These are examples, not facts about you. Many other responses are valid.

1. I get on well with my friends. We don't always **see eye to eye**, but we listen to one another.
2. My hometown has a lively market and lovely parks. Visitors can **have a whale of a time** exploring them.
3. I try to be punctual. If a meeting starts at nine, I aim to arrive at nine **on the dot**.
4. I chose computing because I enjoy solving problems. It took me a while to **find my feet**, but I soon became more confident.
5. My parents are caring and supportive. They can be **set in their ways** about some routines, though.
6. I revised regularly and sometimes **burned the midnight oil** before an exam. Planning my study time helped me most.
7. I applied to five universities. I felt **down in the dumps** after the first rejection, but later I received two offers.
8. I had dinner with my family. I was **over the moon** when a friend surprised me with a visit.

### D

1. **B - her.** The possessive refers to *she*.
2. **A - caught.** Use past simple with *yesterday*.
3. **C - on.** Learn *see eye to eye on a topic*.
4. **B - pie.** The fixed expression has no article before *pie*.
5. **C - call.** *Let's* takes the base form.
6. **A - pull.** *Should* takes the base form.
7. **B - under.** Keep the fixed preposition.
8. **C - thrown.** Present perfect uses the past participle.

### Personal prompts in IELTS Common Expressions - possible answers

1. I was over the moon when I passed my driving test.
2. It took me two months to find my feet in my first job.
3. I sometimes burn the midnight oil when I have an important deadline.
4. Horror films aren't my cup of tea because I find them stressful.
5. I once missed the boat when I waited too long to register for a course.
