# Reporting Passive

Source: [[Sources and Reading Notes#S4 - Making time work|S4]], p. 1, article cards 1, 3, 6, and 7. This is an additional grammar connection drawn from the visible article.

Use this structure to report what people say, think, or believe without naming them individually.

**Pattern:** `subject + is/are + said/thought/believed + to + base verb`

Source fragment: “Time blocking is said to help...”

## Equivalent ways of reporting

New examples:

- **People say that** time blocking helps developers focus.
- **It is said that** time blocking helps developers focus.
- **Time blocking is said to help** developers focus.

The third form makes *time blocking* the main topic. The reporting expression does **not** make the claim a proven fact.

| Reporting phrase | Meaning | New example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| is said to | people say that | The routine **is said to reduce** stress. |
| is thought to | people think that | Quiet time **is thought to improve** focus. |
| is believed to | people believe that | Short breaks **are believed to support** recovery. |

## Read a longer sentence in pieces

New example: **Weekly planning is said to help engineers avoid taking on too much work.**

1. **Weekly planning** = the topic.
2. **is said to help** = a reported benefit.
3. **help engineers avoid** = help + object + base verb.
4. **avoid taking on** = avoid + -ing form.
5. **take on work** = accept work or responsibility.

Connections: [[Passive Voice]] → [[Verb Complements and Cause and Result]] → [[Gerunds and Infinitives]] → [[Phrasal Verbs]].

## Common mistakes

- Incorrect: *Time blocking is say to help.* → **is said to help**
- Incorrect: *Time blocking is said help.* → **is said to help**
- Incorrect: *Short breaks is believed to help.* → **Short breaks are believed to help.**

## Passive make - study extension

Active **make someone do** changes to **be made to do** in the passive:

- Active: **The manager made us repeat the test.**
- Passive: **We were made to repeat the test.**

This is different from *is said to help*, but it is another place where changing the structure changes the infinitive pattern.
