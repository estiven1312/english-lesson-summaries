# IELTS Common Expressions

Source: [[Sources and Reading Notes#S6 - Useful idioms for IELTS|S6]], pp. 5-20. [[PDFs/IELTS/Common Expressions/IELTS common idioms.pdf|Open PDF]].

These **48 selected expressions** come from the PDF. Meanings are paraphrased, examples are new, and some source labels have been normalized to standard forms. This is a language study list, not an official IELTS assessment guide. The PDF title uses *idioms* broadly; some items are phrasal verbs or fixed expressions.

Learn **meaning + grammatical pattern + a personal example**. Use an expression when it fits your meaning; avoid putting one into every answer.

## Grammar to notice

- Change the possessive with the person: **I found my feet; she found her feet**.
- Change the tense of the verb: **catch → caught**, **throw → threw → thrown**.
- Keep fixed words: **a piece of cake**, **as easy as pie**, **split hairs**.
- Learn the preposition: **see eye to eye on**, **fill in for**, **take ... with a grain of salt**.
- Adjective-like expressions need a suitable verb: **I am under the weather**, not *I under the weather*.
